Information architecture and UX design for Boston Market's public website — a single‑page rebuild grounded in a user‑centered product taxonomy, a full content inventory, and persona‑driven wireframes.

The Challenge

Boston Market's site had grown into a maze of seasonal specials, catering menus, and family‑meal bundles with no shared logic holding them together. A guest craving a half chicken and someone planning a holiday catering order landed in the same undifferentiated navigation. I led the UX for a full single‑page rebuild — restructuring the product taxonomy, navigation, and content so every meal, from rotisserie to catering, had one clear home.
